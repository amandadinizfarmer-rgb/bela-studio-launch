# Bela Studio Launch

Create a modern, clean and elegant landing page for an online women's course called "Bela Studio – Makeup & Nails Course". The goal is to sell the course. All text must be in English (US). The site must be fully responsive, mobile-first.

VISUAL IDENTITY

- Style: clean, sophisticated, feminine, with lots of white space, rounded corners and soft shadows.

- Color palette:

  • Main background: #FFF8F6 (rosy off-white)

  • Light nude: #F3DCD4

  • Rose: #E8B4B8

  • Dark rose (buttons and highlights): #C47A84

  • Chocolate brown (headings and text): #4A2C2A

  • Soft gold (details and icons): #C9A96E

- Typography: "Playfair Display" for headings and "Poppins" for body text.

- Fully rounded buttons in dark rose with a soft hover effect.

- Subtle fade and slide-in animations on scroll.

IMAGES

Use realistic, high-quality photos (generate them or use stock sources like Unsplash):

- Beautiful, diverse women wearing natural and glam makeup.

- Close-ups of women's hands with flawless nails: French manicure, nude, rose, delicate nail art, gel nails.

- Close-ups of women's feet with an elegant, polished pedicure.

- Neatly arranged makeup and manicure tools (brushes, palettes, nail polish, nippers).

All images should match the nude/rose palette.

PAGE STRUCTURE

1. Sticky header: "Bela Studio" logo, links (About, Modules, Reviews, Pricing, FAQ) and an "Enroll Now" button.

2. Hero: a strong headline such as "Master Professional Makeup & Nails and Turn Your Passion Into Income". Short subheadline about learning from scratch, at your own pace, with a certificate. Primary button "Start Learning Today" and secondary button "See the Modules". Next to it, a striking image
